ldaps:// is encrypted from the start. ldap:// is not, unless StartTLS is ticked — and without it every password typed crosses the network in the clear.

Stored in EasyWAF's database, as certificate keys are. Give this account the right to search and nothing else.

The search that finds one person. {user} is the name they type. It is also the only say over who may sign in: add a group to it and only that group can — (&(uid={user})(memberOf=cn=staff,ou=groups,dc=example,dc=com)). For Active Directory the name is sAMAccountName.

The attribute of a person's entry that lists their groups. When set, the application is told them, beside the name.
