Everything that arrives from outside, in two stages. A fetch reads the channel, checks the signature over its manifest before reading anything from it, checks every file against the hash that signed manifest gives, and puts what survives in the mirror beside the database. Applying is what puts something in force.
IP lists and the country database apply as they arrive — their value is their freshness. Rule sets wait for you: a new pattern can refuse traffic that was fine yesterday, on every site using the policy.
