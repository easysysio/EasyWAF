Every alias is served by this site, with the same upstream, policy, ports and headers — which is the point: two names for one application used to mean two sites kept in step by hand, and any divergence was a bug that appeared on only one of the names.
A hostname can belong to one site only. Wildcards are not supported: the Host header is matched exactly, and a wildcard certificate needs a DNS-01 challenge EasyWAF does not implement.
A certificate must cover every name, or a browser reaching the site by an uncovered one gets a warning. Requesting one from this page asks for all of them at once.

Only matters with more than one backend, and only for a backend that keeps something in memory: a session, an in-process cache. Without it a client's requests are spread across the pool, and the symptom is a user logged out at random rather than anything that looks like a proxy problem. The client is pinned by a signed cookie, not by its address — many clients share one address behind NAT.
