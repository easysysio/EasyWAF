Before you request

Validation is HTTP-01: the CA connects to the domain over plain HTTP and asks for a token EasyWAF serves. For that to succeed:

  • the domain must resolve to this host from the public internet, not just on your network;
  • this host must be reachable on port 80 — the CA always connects there and cannot be pointed elsewhere;
  • if something else holds port 80, it has to forward /.well-known/acme-challenge/ here.

There is nothing to configure on your backend. EasyWAF answers the challenge itself, and does so even when the site is disabled or its upstream is down — which is usually when you need it.

Certificates last 90 days. EasyWAF renews them automatically once 30 days remain and shows what renewal is doing on each certificate's page.

The certificate is stored but not attached to anything. Assign it to a site under Sites — or use Request Certificate there to issue and assign in one step.
